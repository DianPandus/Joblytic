About the role

We are looking for a proactive, analytical, and commercially minded Business Analyst to support our international business growth in the instant noodle industry across North Africa, the Middle East, and Southeast Europe. This role goes beyond reporting: you will turn data into actionable insights, develop sales projections, identify market opportunities, and work directly with distributors and consumers to drive business growth and commercial execution across these markets. Fresh graduates and candidates with 1–3 years of relevant experience are welcome to apply.

Key responsibilities

Business Analysis & Forecasting: Analyze sales, product movement, pricing, distribution, and market trends; develop forecasts and projections.

Performance Monitoring: Track sales targets, distribution coverage, and KPIs; identify gaps, risks, and growth opportunities, and recommend actions.

Market Intelligence: Monitor competitors, consumer behavior, and market dynamics to support commercial strategies and expansion.

Field Engagement: Visit markets and outlets, interact with consumers, observe distribution activities, and validate data with firsthand insights.

Distributor Collaboration: Communicate confidently with distributors, review performance, identify challenges, and follow up on action plans.

Reporting & Presentation: Prepare reports and presentations that translate data into clear insights, projections, and recommendations.

Cross-functional Collaboration: Coordinate with international sales, marketing, supply chain, and other teams to support business planning and execution.

Requirements

Min bachelor's degree in Business, Economics, Statistics, Finance, Management, or a related field.

Fresh graduates are welcome; 1–3 years' experience in business/sales analysis, commercial planning, market research, or FMCG is an advantage.

Strong interest in numbers, data, commercial performance, and business drivers.

Intermediate Excel skills or above, including data management, trend analysis, reporting, and forecasting.

Strong analytical and critical-thinking skills; able to challenge assumptions and turn findings into actionable recommendations.

Proactive, results-oriented, and commercially driven, with initiative and follow-through.

Confident communication and presentation skills; able to engage professionally with distributors, consumers, and internal stakeholders.

Willing and able to conduct frequent field visits and travel as required.

Willing to undertake overseas assignments, relocate, and be stationed in any country within the assigned regions, as required by business needs.

Adaptable and independent, with the ability to work effectively across cultures and different business environments.
